Americans in four states had measures on their midterm ballots to legalize cannabis, whether for recreational or medical use. With enough votes in to call the results, Michigan, Utah and Missouri join the 30 states that have legalized cannabis either for medical, recreational or both usages. Specifically, Michigan joins as the 10th state to legalize recreational cannabis, and Utah and Missouri join the other 31 states that currently allow medical marijuana.
